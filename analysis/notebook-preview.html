## Context & Methods

Audience: a retail client and technical reviewer. Questions cover recorded merchandise value, comparable months, concentration, known-customer return purchasing, adjustments, and source quality.

### Key Assumptions

GBP decimal line amounts follow `docs/metrics.md`. All original coordinates remain distinct. January–November 2010/2011 are comparable calendar intervals; December 2010 overlap and partial December 2011 are excluded from that comparison. Unknown customer IDs remain in sales totals but not customer cohorts. First observed paid purchase is not acquisition. Missing calendar dates remain unavailable; no zero-sales assumption is made. The 7-observed-day block bootstrap is descriptive sensitivity with seed 20261116, 1,000 repetitions.

## Takeaways

Reconcile the overlapping export period with source-system line IDs, confirm business rules and coverage, and prioritize known segment concentration and quality exceptions for investigation. Customer retention has known-ID selection and left-truncation limits. Inventory, costs, promotion exposure and settlements are absent; no profit, causal marketing effect or realized savings is claimed. The client-readable interpretation is `docs/findings.md`.
